# RiskPrice: Underwriting & Premium Adequacy Intelligence

A SQL underwriting analytics system that identifies policies where **risk and premium may be out of alignment**, prioritizes pricing reviews, compares underwriting risk with claims behavior, and shows how the customer portfolio is distributed across risk segments.

The dataset is synthetic and contains **3,000 insurance policies** across Auto, Property, Life, and Health.
